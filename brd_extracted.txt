BUSINESS REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT
1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
The proposed application is a cloud-based, multi-tenant SaaS platform for managing the complete lifecycle of workplace organisation and 5S implementation.
The solution should go significantly beyond conventional checklist-based 5S audit applications.
The platform shall enable organisations to:
•Digitally define workplace standards.
•Establish area-wise ownership and accountability.
•Conduct 5S audits using mobile devices.
•Capture photographic and other evidence.
•Identify and track abnormalities.
•Manage Red Tags.
•Assign corrective and preventive actions.
•Monitor action closure.
•Establish visual workplace standards.
•Compare present workplace conditions with approved standards.
•Analyse recurring issues.
•Track 5S maturity.
•Benchmark areas, departments and plants.
•Recognise high-performing teams.
•Sustain 5S through systematic governance.
•Use AI to assist auditors, managers and leadership.
•Provide management with conversational and predictive insights.
The long-term objective is to position the application not merely as a 5S Audit Tool, but as a: Workplace Organisation & Operational Discipline Platform
The solution shall support organisations ranging from single-factory SMEs to enterprises operating hundreds of sites.
2. BUSINESS BACKGROUND
5S initiatives commonly fail after the initial implementation phase because organisations struggle with:
•sustaining workplace standards;
•lack of clear area ownership;
•inconsistent audit practices;
•subjective scoring;
•ineffective action follow-up;
•repeat observations;
•inadequate visual standards;
•lack of management visibility;
•excessive dependence on Excel, paper and photographs;
•weak accountability;
•limited employee involvement;
•lack of cross-location benchmarking;
•inadequate trend analysis;
•failure to identify deterioration early.
Most existing systems digitise the audit form but do not address the complete lifecycle required to sustain workplace discipline.
The proposed SaaS application intends to close this gap.

7. 5S FRAMEWORK
The system shall support the conventional 5S methodology:
S1 – Sort: Identify unnecessary items and remove them.
S2 – Set in Order: Organise necessary items for safe and efficient usage.
S3 – Shine: Maintain cleanliness and identify abnormalities.
S4 – Standardise: Define standards for maintaining S1–S3.
S5 – Sustain: Establish discipline and continuously maintain standards.
Customers should optionally be able to extend the framework to:
•6S – Safety

BRULE-02 - Every audit must reference an approved template version.
BRULE-03 - Users may only access authorised locations.
BRULE-04
Audit submissions must retain immutable historical answers.
BRULE-05
AI recommendations require human confirmation before becoming transactional records.
BRULE-06
An audit cannot normally be completed with unanswered mandatory questions.
BRULE-07
Critical observations shall trigger immediate escalation.
BRULE-08
Action closure may require evidence based on customer configuration.
BRULE-09
All score overrides shall be logged.
BRULE-10
A suspended tenant shall not create new transactions.
