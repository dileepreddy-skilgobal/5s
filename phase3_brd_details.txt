24. MOBILE AUDIT EXECUTION
The mobile audit screen should minimise navigation.
Typical auditor workflow:
Scan QR
→
Open Area
→
View Standard
→
Answer Questions
→
Capture Evidence
→
Create Observation
→
Assign Severity
→
Submit Audit
25. OFFLINE CAPABILITY
Mobile applications should support offline audit execution.
Offline mode shall support:
•downloading assigned audits;
•checklist completion;
•photo capture;
•observation recording;
•action creation.
Data shall synchronise automatically when connectivity returns.
26. EVIDENCE CAPTURE
Audit evidence may include:
•photographs;
•short video;
•voice notes;
•attachments;
•text comments.
Metadata should capture:
•timestamp;
•user;
•location if permitted;
•audit reference.
27. PHOTO ANNOTATION
Auditors shall be able to mark:
•arrows;
•circles;
•boxes;
•text annotations
directly on photographs.
28. OBSERVATION MANAGEMENT
Every non-conformance may create an Observation.
Observation fields:
•Observation ID
•Audit
•Location
•5S Category
•Description
•Evidence
•Severity
•Risk
•Assigned To
•Target Date
•Status
•Root Cause
•Corrective Action
•Verified By
•Closure Evidence
29. SEVERITY CLASSIFICATION
Example:
•Minor
•Moderate
•Major
•Critical
Tenants shall configure severity rules.
30. CORRECTIVE ACTION MANAGEMENT
Actions may arise from:
•audit observations;
•abnormalities;
•red tags;
•management reviews;
•AI suggestions.
Workflow:
Open
→
Assigned
→
In Progress
→
Pending Verification
→
Verified
→
Closed
31. ACTION SLA
The system shall calculate target closure dates based on:
•severity;
•area;
•customer configuration.
Example:
Critical – 24 hours
Major – 3 days
Moderate – 7 days
Minor – 15 days
32. ACTION ESCALATION
Escalation rules may include: In app and Email
T-2 days → reminder
Due date → owner alert -
+2 days → department head
+5 days → plant head
+10 days → 5S coordinator
Rules shall be configurable.
33. CLOSURE EVIDENCE
Actions should not normally be closed without:
•closure comments;
•after photograph.
Customers may configure exceptions.
34. ACTION VERIFICATION
The person performing the action should not necessarily verify the action.
The system shall support independent verification.
35. REOPENING ACTIONS
Verifier may reopen actions where corrective actions are inadequate.
History shall be maintained.
36. RED TAG MANAGEMENT
The system shall provide dedicated Red Tag functionality.
Red Tag fields: Option to generate or print red tag from system should be allowed
•Tag Number – with option for unique QR code tracking
•Item
•Description
•Quantity
•Area
•Photograph
•Reason
•Tagged By
•Tag Date
•Owner
•Decision
•Target Disposition Date
•Disposition
•Closure Date
•Purchase value (if applicable)
131. WHITE LABELLING
Enterprise customers may optionally configure:
•logo;
•application colour;
•email branding;
•custom domain.
132. SEARCH
Global search shall support searching by:
•audit number;
•observation;
•area;
•action;
•Red Tag;
•employee.
133. ATTACHMENT SECURITY
Attachment access shall require authorised application sessions.
Sensitive files should not be exposed through unrestricted public URLs.
134. SYSTEM MONITORING
Platform operations shall monitor:
•CPU;
•memory;
•database health;
•queue health;
•API errors;
•background jobs;
•storage;
•failed notifications.
135. APPLICATION LOGGING
Logs shall include:
•application errors;
•integration failures;
•background job errors;
•AI failures.
Customer data should be minimised in system logs.
136. OBSERVABILITY
Use:
•metrics;
•logs;
•tracing
for production support.
